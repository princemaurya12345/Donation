Campaign Updates

Stay informed about our progress and milestones

Milestone Reached: 75% Funded!

New

December 15, 2024

We're thrilled to announce that we've reached 75% of our funding goal! Thanks to the incredible generosity of our donors, we're on track to break ground on the first school in January. The community is buzzing with excitement!

Site Selected for First School

December 8, 2024

After careful evaluation, we've selected the site for our first school in the Mwanza region of Tanzania. The location was chosen for its accessibility and proximity to the communities that need it most.

Partnership with Local NGO

November 28, 2024

We've partnered with Education for All Tanzania (EFAT) to ensure sustainable operations and local expertise. This partnership will help us maximize the impact of every dollar donated.

Campaign Launch!

November 1, 2024

Today marks the beginning of our journey to build schools and change lives. We're launching with a goal of $250,000 to build 3 schools and provide education to over 5,000 children. Join us in making this dream a reality!

Everything you need to know about donating

Yes! HopeRise is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You'll receive a receipt via email for your records.

90% of every donation goes directly to our programs - building schools, providing supplies, and supporting teachers. The remaining 10% covers essential administrative costs and fundraising efforts to sustain our mission.

Absolutely! We accept donations from anywhere in the world. Our payment processors support multiple currencies and international credit/debit cards. PayPal also facilitates international transfers.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) through Stripe, as well as PayPal and direct bank transfers. All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.

Yes! During the payment process, you'll have the option to make your donation recurring (monthly, quarterly, or annually). Recurring donors receive special recognition and exclusive updates about the project.

Unlike some platforms, we use a flexible funding model. All donations received will be used for our education programs regardless of whether we reach our full goal. We'll adjust our plans to maximize impact with available funds.
